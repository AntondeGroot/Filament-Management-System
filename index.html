Swatches

Every colour you own, sorted by hue. Rolls you buy land here automatically and stay after the last one runs out — so this doubles as a record of what you used to have.

Machines and storage

Name these whatever you call them — AMS, MMU, CFS, "the beast". A printer holds the spools mounted on the machine itself: one for an external spool holder, four if they sit on top. Weeks dry is how long a spool keeps before this place flags it for drying — around 4 weeks out in the open or on a printer, and much longer in a sealed box with desiccant. Set it to whatever matches your climate; a dryer has no countdown, since nothing goes damp inside one.

Drying intervals

How long a spool keeps in each kind of place before it gets flagged for drying. Set once per type — every box you own uses the same number. Tune it to your climate: a damp garage wants less, a dehumidified room more, and if you live somewhere genuinely dry, switch track drying off and every trace of it disappears — or set a single type to never if it's just your boxes you trust.

Reminders

A nudge when spools are past their drying window, at most once a day. Your browser has to grant permission first, and it will ask the moment you tick this.

Drying due

Reminders arrive while the page is open. Add this to your Android home screen and Chrome will also check in the background every so often — background checks inactive. Chrome decides how often; a couple of times a day is typical.
